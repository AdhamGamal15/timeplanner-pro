About TimePlanner Pro

TimePlanner Pro is your personal companion for staying on top of everyday life.

Between work, school, appointments, and personal goals, it's easy for small tasks to slip through the cracks. TimePlanner Pro gives you one simple, friendly place to write down what needs to get done, when it needs to happen, and why it matters.

Add a task in seconds, give it a category so it's easy to find later, and let TimePlanner Pro keep an eye on the clock for you. When a deadline is approaching or has arrived, you'll get a gentle nudge so nothing important is forgotten.

Your tasks stay with you every time you come back, and if you keep the app open in more than one window, changes you make in one will show up in the other right away. You can edit a task the moment your plans change, and remove anything you no longer need with a single click.

Whether you're juggling a busy week at work, keeping track of study sessions, or simply trying to remember your errands, TimePlanner Pro is built to make planning feel a little lighter and a lot more organized.
